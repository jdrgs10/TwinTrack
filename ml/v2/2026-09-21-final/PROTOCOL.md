## Production contract proposed for integration

Target: an **experimental estimate of the percentage score (0–100) on the next scheduled, individually graded written quiz/test/exam in a student-selected subject, occurring 1–7 local calendar days after the prediction cutoff**. Exclude same-day assessments, group work, coursework, unknown dates and incomparable scales. If multiple assessments in that subject are scheduled, use the earliest eligible one; do not skip an earlier same-day assessment to choose a later one. If no future assessment in the next seven days is known, abstain. Do not forecast beyond seven days. The seven-day limit is a product scope assumption, not an empirically validated horizon. Days-to-assessment is eligibility metadata, not an eighth predictor; the simulator assumes no horizon-specific mean effect and does not validate this assumption.

Previous score: most recent earlier individually graded assessment in that same subject, expressed as points earned / possible ×100, with a valid positive denominator, dated within the preceding90 local calendar days (strictly before prediction day). Require same subject for this scoped model; do not silently use another subject. If missing/incomparable/undated, abstain. No student/school identifiers required. Assessment subject/date/scale and manual source-day metadata are local eligibility metadata, not Ridge features. Legacy undated manual values/previous score require reconfirmation, never inferred dates.

Health inputs unchanged: sleep yesterday local noon→min(now,today noon), union accessible asleep hours; steps/exercise previous completed local calendar day count/minutes; RHR latest valid finite-positive sample in last72 elapsed hours, timestamp retained. Study = **previous completed local calendar day's deliberate outside-class study for the selected subject, minutes**, including academic screen use. Screen = **previous completed local calendar day's recreational/non-study active screen-use minutes**, aggregated by elapsed time, not summing simultaneous devices; exclude schoolwork/study and avoid double counting study. Both dated to the same yesterday used by activity. Enter/reconfirm after midnight; absence is missing, explicit0 is valid. Prior-day entry is not today's cumulative total or a multi-day average.

What-If overrides precisely those captured source-window totals. +30 study means a hypothetical yesterday with30 more minutes for the selected subject, not a guaranteed benefit tomorrow. Keep read-only scenarios and all seven features. Health-derived values are not edited in real state. Previous score/RHR overrides are hypothetical background inputs, not habits to optimize.
